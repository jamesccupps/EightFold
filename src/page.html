      Controls

      Click a key to change it, then press the new key. Esc cancels, Backspace clears.

      Touch: tap a side or swipe sideways to turn, swipe up to flip, swipe down to jump. A mouse can swipe too; a plain click does nothing.

      Options

      For testers

      Tuning tries other speeds and timings (those runs are not scored). Play data shows what is recorded, and switches it off.

      Tuning

      Try other values for how the game feels, then play with them. The view changes as you drag. Runs with changed pace, moves or forgiveness are not scored.

      When it feels right, send these values back and they become the game's defaults.
